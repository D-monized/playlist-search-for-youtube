# Privacy Policy: Playlist Search for YouTube

_Effective date: November 7, 2026_

Playlist Search for YouTube ("the extension") is a browser extension that helps you find YouTube videos in your playlists, browsing history and bookmarks. This policy explains what data the extension accesses and what it does with it.

## Summary

The extension works entirely inside your browser. It does **not** collect, store, sell or send your data to the developer or anyone else. It has no servers, no analytics, no ads, and no account or sign-in.

## Data the extension accesses

| Data | Why it is accessed | When |
|---|---|---|
| **Browsing history** (page addresses, titles, last visit time) | To find YouTube videos you have watched that match your search in the toolbar popup | Only when you open the popup or type a search |
| **Bookmarks** (addresses and titles) | To find YouTube videos you have bookmarked that match your search | Only when you open the popup or type a search |
| **YouTube page content** (video titles shown in a playlist on youtube.com) | To hide playlist videos that do not match what you type in the "Search this playlist" box | Only on www.youtube.com playlist pages and the playlist panel beside a video |

The extension only shows results that link to YouTube videos. Other history and bookmark entries are read by the browser's search function, but are discarded immediately and never displayed or kept.

## How the data is used

The data above is used only to show you search results inside the extension's popup or on the YouTube page you are viewing. It is processed in your browser's memory and discarded when you close the popup or leave the page.

## Storage

The extension does not save any of your data. It does not use cookies, local storage, sync storage or any database.

## Sharing and transfer

The extension makes **no network requests**. Your history, bookmarks and page content never leave your device. The developer never receives your data, and it is not sold, shared or transferred to any third party, used for advertising, or used to determine creditworthiness or for lending.

The use of information received from browser APIs adheres to the [Chrome Web Store User Data Policy](https://developer.chrome.com/docs/webstore/program-policies/user-data-faq), including the Limited Use requirements.

## Permissions

- **history**: search your browsing history for YouTube videos.
- **bookmarks**: search your bookmarks for YouTube videos.
- **Access to www.youtube.com**: add the search box to YouTube playlists.

The extension never modifies or deletes your history or bookmarks.

## Changes to this policy

If the extension's handling of data changes, this policy will be updated and the effective date above will change.

## Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the support link on the extension's Chrome Web Store page.
